Background

"Multi, multimedia" isn't a redundancy — it's a job description. The work moves between print, screen, and physical fabrication depending on what the project actually needs. This has made focus difficult and the toolkit unusually wide.

Tools of the Trade

Cinema 4D for 3D. Adobe for most everything flat. Barge contact cement and X-Acto blades for things that need to exist in a room. Other tools as required, none particularly precious about it.
